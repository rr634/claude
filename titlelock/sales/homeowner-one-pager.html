Always Watching. Always Protected.

Florida is one of the most-targeted states for home title theft — criminals forge a deed to your home, then sell it or borrow against it, and most owners don't find out for months. DeedGuard's AI re-scans your deed and title in the public record every few days and alerts you — in plain English — the instant anything changes.

Why it matters: The owner's title policy you got at closing generally won't cover forgery that happens after closing (Exclusion 3(d)). Catching a bad filing fast — and knowing what to do — is what actually protects you.
