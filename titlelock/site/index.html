The threat

Your Florida home's title is a public document — and that's the problem.

Florida's vacant lots, second homes, and snowbird properties are among the nation's most-targeted. Deeds are filed at the county, and a fraudster only needs to forge a transfer, file it, and on paper your home now belongs to someone else. They borrow against it, sell it, or evict tenants — and most owners don't find out for months. The owner's title policy you bought at closing generally won't cover forgery that happens after (Exclusion 3(d)) — so catching it fast is everything.

DeedGuard's AI watches the record for you and flags trouble in plain English — with a Florida attorney on call, and an optional title-insurance add-on where it's available.

Questions

What you should know.

How is DeedGuard different from Home Title Lock and other "title locks"?
Three ways. Our AI re-scans the public record every few days and explains each filing in plain English — so you know what actually happened, not just that something did. A real Florida real estate attorney is on call to help you act. And pricing is transparent with no lock-in — cancel anytime. Where it's genuinely available, we can also add an optional title-insurance endorsement.
How often do you check my title?
Our AI re-scans your property's public record every few days — continuously, not once a year — and compares every new filing against your baseline. You can also request an on-demand check anytime.
Is DeedGuard insurance?
No. DeedGuard is an AI-powered monitoring and alert service with attorney support — it is not insurance and is not a substitute for title insurance. Where it's available for sale, we can separately help you add a real title-insurance forgery endorsement (ALTA 49 / 49.1) through a licensed underwriter, as an optional add-on billed on its own.
What's the optional insurance add-on?
In states where it's sold, the ALTA 49 / 49.1 forgery endorsement adds real title-insurance coverage for post-closing deed/mortgage forgery to your owner's title policy, through a licensed title agent/underwriter. We'll tell you whether it's available for your property and connect you — it's priced and billed separately from your DeedGuard subscription.
Doesn't my title insurance already cover this?
The standard owner's policy only covers forgery that happened before your policy date — for 20+ years it has excluded post-closing forgery (Exclusion 3(d)). That's the gap home title theft exploits, and it's why ongoing monitoring matters. The optional ALTA 49 / 49.1 add-on can cover it going forward where available.
What happens when you detect something suspicious?
You get an instant alert by email (and text on Defend and Premier plans) with a plain-English explanation of exactly what was recorded and what it means. On attorney plans, a Florida real estate attorney helps you verify whether it's legitimate and, if not, dispute the filing and notify the county recorder.
How do you monitor my property?
We match your address to its parcel in the official county land records and snapshot the current, legitimate deed and title. From then on, our AI re-scans every few days and checks each new document against your baseline, scoring it for risk.
Is my personal information safe?
Yes. We use your address only to look up public land records, and your contact details only to send your alerts. We never sell your data. Property records themselves are already public — we simply watch them for you.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. There are no contracts. Cancel from your dashboard whenever you like and monitoring stops at the end of your billing period.
