Privacy Policy

Last updated:

DeedGuard USA ("DeedGuard," "we," "us") provides an AI-powered property-records monitoring and alert service. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Property details you give us (address, and for monitoring, parcel/owner information).
  • Contact details (email, and phone for text alerts if you provide one).
  • Account & billing data processed by our payment provider (Stripe); we do not store full card numbers.
  • Public records relating to your property, which we retrieve from government sources to monitor your title.
  • Usage data (basic, privacy-respecting analytics about how the site is used).

How we use it

  • To monitor the public record for your property and send you alerts.
  • To provide, bill for, and support your subscription.
  • To respond to your requests and, on eligible plans, connect you with attorney support.
  • To improve the service and measure interest in our offerings.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information. We use your address only to look up public land records, and your contact details only to provide the service and send your alerts. We share data only with service providers that help us operate (e.g., payment, email, hosting, analytics) under appropriate terms, or where required by law.

Cookies & analytics

We use lightweight analytics to understand site usage. If advertising pixels are enabled, they may set cookies to measure ad performance; you can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention & security

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, and we use reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you can cancel your subscription at any time. To make a request, email rr@richardrosalaw.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email rr@richardrosalaw.com.
