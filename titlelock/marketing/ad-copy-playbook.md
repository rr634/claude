# DeedGuard USA — Ad Copy & Launch Playbook

**Positioning:** AI-powered home title monitoring for Florida homeowners — smarter than a basic
"title lock," with a real estate attorney on call.
**Tagline:** *Always Watching. Always Protected.*
**Landing page:** https://deedguardusa.com (primary CTA: **free property check**)
**Creatives:** in `marketing/creatives/renders/` — `ad-square-fear`, `ad-square-vs`,
`ad-square-price`, `ad-story`, `ad-landscape`. OG share card: `site/assets/og-cover.png`.

> Compliance guardrails for every ad: DeedGuard is **monitoring, not insurance** — never imply it
> prevents fraud or pays claims. "Free check" must be genuinely free. Keep insurance mentions as
> "optional, where available." It's attorney-adjacent, so avoid guarantees of legal outcomes.

## Meta (Facebook / Instagram) — 3 ad angles

### Angle A — Fear hook  → pair with `ad-square-fear` / `ad-story`
**Primary text:** Your home's deed is a public document. In Florida, criminals forge a single
signature, record a fake deed, and borrow against — or sell — a house they never owned. Most owners
don't find out for months. DeedGuard's AI watches your title around the clock and tells you the
instant anything changes — with a real Florida attorney on call. Run a free title check today.
**Headline:** Is your home's title still in your name?
**Description:** AI title monitoring + a Florida attorney. Always Watching. Always Protected.
**CTA button:** Learn More

### Angle B — Vs. "title lock"  → pair with `ad-square-vs`
**Primary text:** A basic "title lock" just fires a scary alert and leaves you to figure it out
alone. DeedGuard is different: our AI re-scans the public record every few days, explains exactly
what was filed in plain English, and puts a real Florida real estate attorney on call if something's
wrong. Transparent pricing, cancel anytime.
**Headline:** Smarter than a title lock.
**Description:** AI + a real attorney watching your Florida home's title.
**CTA button:** Get Started

### Angle C — Value / price  → pair with `ad-square-price`
**Primary text:** Protecting your biggest asset shouldn't cost a fortune. DeedGuard watches your
Florida home's deed and title with AI from about $17/month — plain-English alerts, a real estate
attorney on call, and no lock-in. See exactly what's recorded against your home with a free check.
**Headline:** Watch your home's title from $17/mo.
**Description:** AI monitoring + attorney-backed. Cancel anytime.
**CTA button:** Sign Up

**Meta targeting:** Location — Florida (consider metro rings: Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, Tampa,
Orlando, Naples/Ft. Myers). Age 45–75+. Homeowners; likely-to-move = off. Behaviors/interests:
homeownership, real estate, "snowbirds"/seasonal residents, estate planning, AARP, identity-theft
protection (LifeLock/Aura), Nextdoor-style community. Also build a **lookalike** off free-check leads
once you have ~100.

## Launch plan (first 30 days)
1. **Wire the lead form** → email/CRM before any spend (see `site/index.html` `checkForm`).
2. **Install tracking** — Meta Pixel + Google Tag; fire a "Lead" event on free-check submit.
3. **Start small:** $20–40/day Meta (Angles A + B) + $20/day Google Search. One geo (e.g. Broward/
   Palm Beach) first.
4. **Measure:** cost per free-check lead → cost per paid subscription. Kill losers weekly, scale
   winners, expand geos.
5. **Nurture:** auto-email the free-check result + a soft offer; retarget site visitors with
   `ad-square-price`.
6. **Organic/PR flywheel:** the one-pager (`sales/homeowner-one-pager.html`) for realtor/estate-
   attorney referral partners; short Reels using the story creative.

## A/B ideas
- Hook: fear ("steal your home") vs. category ("smarter than a title lock") vs. price ($17/mo).
- CTA: "Free title check" vs. "See what's recorded on your home."
- Landing headline variants against the same ad.
