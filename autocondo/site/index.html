The Idea

Not a storage unit. Not a showroom. A private address for the cars you love — and a room full of people who understand why.

South Florida has the cars, the roads and the weather. What it hasn't had is a place built for them — somewhere you can keep a collection properly, spend a Saturday with it, and pull up to a crowd that cares about the same things you do. The Paddock is that place.

Questions

Good to
know.

Do I actually own my studio?

Yes. Each studio is a separately deeded condominium unit. You can finance it, sell it, lease it within the association rules, or leave it to the next generation, like any other real property.

What do monthly association fees cover?

Building insurance, common-area upkeep, security and monitoring, the clubhouse, landscaping and reserves. Studio electricity and internet are metered and billed to each owner.

Can I work on my cars in the studio?

Light maintenance, detailing and hobby work are welcome. Commercial repair, painting and fuel storage aren't permitted; the association's rules set out the details.

What happens in a hurricane?

Studios are concrete, impact-rated and set above base flood elevation. Ahead of a storm, the team checks every door, generator and drain, and owners get status updates through the app.

Can I store a boat, bike or RV?

Motorcycles, track toys and memorabilia are all at home here. Boats and RVs depend on studio size and door clearance, so ask us about a specific unit.
