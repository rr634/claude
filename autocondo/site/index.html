The Idea

You don't park at 8 AT RIO. You arrive.

Turn off SE 12th Street and a single glass door rises. Behind it is the Hall, one covered, climate-controlled drive aisle lined with eight glass garage doors that light up like display cases. Pull straight into your bay and the door comes down behind you. No sun on the paint, no salt in the air and nobody watching but the other seven.

The Hall

One roof.
One way in.

The courtyard between the two rows is 25 feet wide, exactly what it takes to pull straight into a garage. So we covered it. The open-air walk becomes a private, conditioned hall with a skylight running down its length. You drive the car in, and it stays out of the weather from the moment the gate opens.

01

Skylight Spine

Daylight runs the length of the Hall, through glass specified to filter UV. The cars get the light without the sun.

02

The Turntable

At the north end of the Hall, a turntable spins the car so you leave nose-first. On Hall Nights it becomes the plinth.

03

The Gate

04

Detail Bay & Lounge

05

The Plan

Lined up.
Locked in.

There are two rows of four identical bays, each on a 22′-6″ grid, so the doors line up across the Hall. You enter from SE 12th Street to the south. The turntable, lounge and pool are at the north end. The footprint stays within the zoning setbacks.

Questions

Good to
know.

What exactly is a bay at 8 AT RIO?

It's a private garage residence. The main floor is a 14-foot garage and living space, a mezzanine lounge sits above it, and a glass door opens onto the covered Hall. The project is planned as eight multifamily residential units under the site's RML-25 zoning.

Can I put a lift in?

The bays are planned with about 14 feet of clear height and a slab ready for lift anchors, which suits a four-post stacking lift for most cars. Final lift sizing depends on the cars and the finished design.

Can I work on my cars?

Light maintenance, detailing and hobby work are what the bays are built for. Commercial repair, spray painting and fuel storage won't be permitted, and the association rules will set out the details.

Is the Hall really enclosed?

That's the plan: a covered, climate-controlled hall with a skylight, a single gated entry and mechanical ventilation. The pool terrace at the north end is the only open-air space.

Will I own my bay?

The bays are planned to be sold individually as condominium units, each with its own deed. The final ownership structure and association documents will be set out in the offering materials.

Where is the project in the approval process?

The covered-hall layout refines the conceptual schematics prepared for City development review. Dimensions, materials and amenities may change as the design is finalized and approved.
