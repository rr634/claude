The Idea

Eight loft residences. Each one has a double-height garage right off the living room.

8 AT RIO is a small gated enclave on SE 12th Street in Rio Vista, one of Fort Lauderdale's most established neighborhoods. Two rows of four residences face an open motor court that runs from the gate to a shared pool and cabana. The entire ground floor of each residence is a private garage, rising about 22 feet over half its footprint. The home sits above it on the mezzanine: open loft living with a full kitchen and a full bath, looking down on the cars.

The Residences

Live in the loft.
Park in the gallery.

Each residence is 40 feet deep and 22 or 24 feet wide. The whole ground floor is garage. Over half of it the ceiling rises the full height of the building, about 22 feet, and over the other half the garage runs about 14 feet high under the mezzanine. The home is up on the mezzanine: an open loft with a full kitchen and a full bath, with no walls, in the tradition of a true loft, and a view down onto the cars.

Questions

Good to
know.

What is a loft residence at 8 AT RIO?

It's a home built over a garage. The entire ground floor is a private garage, double height (about 22 feet) over half of it. The living space is on the mezzanine: an open loft with a full kitchen and a full bath. The conceptual site plan proposes eight multifamily residential units under the site's RML-25 zoning.

Is there a separate bedroom?

No. The sleeping area is an open loft without walls, in the tradition of loft living, so the residences are open-plan homes rather than one-bedroom units. The final plans will show the layout in detail.

Can I live there full time?

The residences are designed to be lived in, with a full kitchen, a full bath and a sleeping loft. How the residences may be occupied and used will be governed by the condominium documents.

Will I own my residence?

The intent is to offer the residences individually as condominium units. The ownership structure, budget and association documents will be set out in the offering documents required by Florida law, and those documents, not this website, will govern.

Where is it, and what's there now?

The site is on SE 12th Street in Rio Vista, just south of downtown Fort Lauderdale. The lot is 16,875 SF.

Where is the project in the approval process?

The plans and renderings shown are conceptual and have not been approved by the City of Fort Lauderdale. Dimensions, materials, amenities and the number and size of residences may change as the design moves through review.

Can I customize the interior?

The intent is to allow owners to choose finishes. Specific options, if any, will be set out in the purchase documents.

How do I reserve?

Reservations and contracts aren't being accepted yet. Join the interest list below and we'll contact you when they are.
