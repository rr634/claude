Questions

Straight answers.

How do you come up with your offer?

We look at the property’s income, condition, location, comparable sales, and what it would cost to stabilize. We’ll walk you through our numbers so you can see exactly how we got there.

Am I obligated to accept?

No. Our offer is free and there’s no obligation. If it doesn’t work for you, we can talk about listing the property instead, or we can simply part as friends.

Will my cash offer be lower than market value?

A cash offer trades some price for speed, certainty, and zero commissions. That’s why we show you both options. If listing will put more money in your pocket, we’ll tell you.

Can you buy a property with tenants in place?

Yes. We buy occupied, partly vacant, and fully vacant properties. We’ll handle tenant notices and estoppels at closing.

What if there are liens, code violations, or title problems?

That’s routine for us. We work with experienced title and legal professionals to resolve liens and violations, usually out of the sale proceeds at closing.

Will my tenants or competitors find out I’m selling?

Not from us. Cash sales are handled confidentially. We don’t list the property or contact your tenants without your permission.

Do you work with 1031 exchanges?

Yes. We can time our closing to fit your exchange, and our brokerage can help you find replacement property.
