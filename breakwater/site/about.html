Richard Rosa

A rare combination, under one roof.

Richard Rosa is the founder and principal of Breakwater Capital Partners. He is a Florida real estate attorney, a licensed Florida real estate broker, a mortgage loan originator, and the owner of a title agency — and an active real estate investor across South Florida.

That combination is the point. Resolving a distressed project means clearing title and liens, restructuring debt, negotiating with lenders and receivers, taking control through foreclosure or deed-in-lieu, completing construction, and selling the finished product. Most groups have to assemble that from outside counsel, title companies, brokers, and lenders — slowly, and at a cost. Breakwater holds it internally. That is why it can move quietly, close with certainty, and actually finish what others couldn't.
