Deal Profiles

Three markets. One discipline.

Each profile follows the acquisition box: 50–90% complete at entry, $2M–$15M finished value, a defined control path, and a 25%+ completion spread to all-in basis.

Fort Lauderdale · Broward

Waterfront spec home, maturity default

A deep-water single-family spec stalled at roughly 70% complete when the construction loan hit maturity default and the original developer ran out of runway. Breakwater acquired the note and took title by negotiated deed-in-lieu, cleared two subordinate liens through the in-house title agency, and finished to CO.

  • Distress triggerConstruction-loan maturity default
  • Control pathNote purchase → deed-in-lieu
  • All-in basis~$4.6M
  • Cost to complete~$0.9M
  • Finished value~$6.4M
  • Completion spread~28%

Outcome: completed and sold — ~9 month hold.

Miami Beach · Miami-Dade

Modern waterfront, developer insolvency

A contemporary waterfront residence at ~60% complete with mechanic's liens stacking as the sponsor became insolvent. Rather than force a foreclosure, Breakwater recapitalized the project through a preferred-equity rescue, took operating control, resolved the lien stack, and carried the build to completion.

  • Distress triggerSponsor insolvency · lien stack
  • Control pathPref-equity recap → control
  • All-in basis~$8.2M
  • Cost to complete~$1.8M
  • Finished value~$11.5M
  • Completion spread~27%

Outcome: completed and sold — ~12 month hold.

Palm Beach · Palm Beach Co.

Estate asset in receivership

A luxury residence stranded at ~80% complete inside a court-supervised receivership, with cost overruns and an owner under time pressure. Breakwater bought the asset REO from the lender at a controlled closing, re-scoped the remaining finish work, and brought it to a marketable exit quickly.

  • Distress triggerReceivership · cost overruns
  • Control pathREO purchase from lender
  • All-in basis~$3.1M
  • Cost to complete~$0.5M
  • Finished value~$4.3M
  • Completion spread~26%

Outcome: completed and sold — ~7 month hold.
