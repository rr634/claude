Good questions

Things people usually ask.

How do you figure out your offer?

I look at the rent it brings in, its condition, the neighborhood, and what similar properties have sold for. Then I walk you through the numbers so nothing feels like a mystery.

Do I have to accept?

Not at all. The offer is free, and there’s zero pressure. If it doesn’t work for you, no hard feelings.

Will a cash offer be less than listing it?

Sometimes, yes. A cash sale trades a bit of price for speed, certainty, and no commissions. That’s exactly why I show you both options side by side.

What happens to my tenants?

They can stay. I buy occupied buildings all the time and handle the tenant paperwork at closing.

The property has liens or code violations. Is that a problem?

Nope. Those are usually paid off or resolved at closing, out of the sale. We handle the details with the title company.

Will anyone know I’m selling?

Only if you want them to. Cash sales are completely private. I won’t contact your tenants or advertise the property without your OK.
